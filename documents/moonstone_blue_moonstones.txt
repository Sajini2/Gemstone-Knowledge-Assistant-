Blue moonstones represent the most coveted and valuable commercial grade of moonstone within the gem marketplace. Unlike common white or silvery-grey moonstones, top-tier blue moonstones feature an intense, ethereal electric blue or royal blue adularescent flash floating over a highly transparent, colorless body.

The quality and market value of blue moonstone are evaluated according to three core optical dimensions: body color transparency, schiller intensity, and play of light coverage. The finest specimens display semi-transparent to transparent glass-like bodies free of turbid inclusions, eye-visible fractures, or milky cloudiness. A muddy or opaque body scatter light diffusely, obscuring the sharpness and depth of the floating blue sheen.

The ideal blue adularescent flash exhibits high color saturation and three-dimensional depth, moving fluidly across 180 degrees of viewing angles as the gemstone shifts. Specimens exhibiting a three-dimensional blue glow centered over a crystal-clear body are often referred to in trade circles as "Royal Blue Moonstones."

Due to high global demand and limited natural yields, fine blue moonstones are subject to market imitation. Glass, synthetic spinel, and clear labradorite cabochons are sometimes mislabeled. Advanced gemological evaluation using refractive index testing (1.518-1.526) and specific gravity measurements (2.56-2.59) confirms authentic orthoclase feldspar identity.
